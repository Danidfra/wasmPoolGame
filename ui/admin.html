LN Pool

Run a pool hall: players buy in, the winner takes the pot.

Hall

The hall fee is the share of each pot you keep. Lightning routing fees are a separate network cost, paid from this wallet whenever a prize is sent.

    Winners are paid from this wallet without you being present, so LNbits asks you to approve background payments up to two largest stakes.

    LNbits only sends a payout when the wallet holds the prize plus a reserve for routing fees: by default 2 sats, or 1% of the payout if that is more. A hall fee does not guarantee that: with a small fee or a small pot it leaves less than the reserve. Keeping enough in this wallet beyond the pots is up to you. A payout the wallet cannot cover is not sent: the match is flagged below, and the player tries again once you have added funds.

To settle by hand, close it first, then look in the wallet's payments for an outgoing "LN Pool payout" with this match's id. If there is one that has not failed, the player has been paid or is being paid. Otherwise pay or refund them yourself. "Unconfirmed" means a payment was started and its result never reached LN Pool, usually because it took longer than LNbits allows one call: check the wallet's payments before doing anything.
